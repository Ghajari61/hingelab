# Template library: sources and licences

Files under `public/templates/` are built by `node scripts/build-templates.mjs`.

## HingeLab originals

All templates except the two below were drawn for HingeLab with its own Studio shapes, Live Effects and
generators (clean-room; no third-party geometry). They are part of HingeLab.

## Designs from the owner's collection

### Ceramic tile frame — `files/ceramic-tile-frame/`

The owner's own design (exported from xTool Creative Space, no author or licence mark). Bundled as the
owner's work; the US Letter sheet edge was left out and the colours mapped to cut layers.

### KV-1 tank model — `files/kv1-tank/`

- Title: Carter's lasercut tank model – KV-1 (motorizable)
- Author: JackCarter
- Source: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2449310 (published 23 July 2017)
- Licence: Creative Commons – Attribution (CC BY), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Changes by HingeLab: colours mapped to HingeLab's cut layers (black → cut, red → score), text notes
  removed from the drawing (their content is in the template description), file metadata removed.
  Geometry and scale are unchanged.

The credit is also shown in the template gallery and written into each SVG file.
