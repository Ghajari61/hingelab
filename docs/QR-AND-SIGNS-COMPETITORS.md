# QR Code generator: what competitors offer and what HingeLab added

Research for the QR Code generator and the generators around it. Ideas were taken from public product pages and help articles. No designs, files or brand artwork were copied, and no brand logos were drawn.

## Online QR designers (QRCode Monkey, Uniqode, Scanova)

These tools let people style a code beyond plain squares:

- **Body patterns:** shapes for the data modules, such as rounded, dots, diamonds, connected circles and bars.
- **Eye frame and eye ball shapes:** the corner finder patterns, chosen separately.
- **Frames with a call to action:** for example "Scan me".
- **A logo in the middle.**
- **Template galleries.**

**HingeLab now has:**

- Eight module styles: square, rounded, dots, connected, diamonds, small squares, vertical bars and horizontal bars.
- Five eye frames: square, rounded, circle, leaf and cushion.
- Six eye centres: square, rounded, circle, leaf, diamond and star.
- Every pairing marked safe is decoded in the tests.
- A warning, with a fix, for the pairings that some scanners reject. Detectors also measure each eye corner to corner.

## Laser software (xTool Creative Space / Studio, LightBurn)

- **Their QR tools:** text, a Wi-Fi login or a contact card, plus the error-correction level. There are no stands, plate shapes or module styles.
- **HingeLab now has:** File ▸ Insert QR Code in Studio, with the designer styles. HingeLab's QR generator already covered more content types: email, SMS, phone, and now WhatsApp, map locations and calendar events.

## Laser-made QR signs (Etsy, Atomm/xTool project gallery, menu-stand shops, maker blogs)

**Common products:**

- Review signs with five stars.
- "Scan or tap to pay" counter signs with an NFC tag behind an icon.
- Wi-Fi stands.
- Social "follow us" signs.
- Menu table tents.
- Wedding photo-share signs.
- Pet tags.
- Plant markers.
- Wall plaques on standoffs.

**Common construction:**

- An L-shaped stand, or a sign in a slotted base.
- Feet with slots.
- Mounting holes for standoffs.
- Two-colour acrylic, or masked wood for contrast.
- A 2 in (50 mm) or larger code for laser-cut codes.

**HingeLab now has:**

- **Stands and mounts:** a slotted base (one to three layers), two slotted feet that lean the sign back, a tent card folded on a living hinge with the code on both faces, and two or four wall holes.
- **Header pictures:** stars, menu, coffee, Wi-Fi, camera, map pin, payment card, contactless tap, chat, phone, calendar, and the shared motifs.
- **Plate shapes:** arch, hexagon, octagon, shield, speech bubble, scalloped, ticket, dog bone and plant stake.
- **Presets** for each product above.
- **Assembly notes,** including the NFC tag tip.
- **Checks:** the stand tipping over, and holes in the quiet zone.

## Other generators

- **Table Numbers:** an optional QR code engraved under each number. Put `{n}` in the link and every table gets its own code ("menu?table=4"). Tent cards carry it on both faces, and raised numbers keep it engraved. It is checked for module size.
